# My agent: plant-care-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts and home gardeners care for, diagnose, and expand their indoor/outdoor garden with a catalog of plants, care schedules, and nursery inventory.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's home environment (light levels, humidity, pets) and plant collection care history
- Tools: Look up plant care requirements/toxicity, check nursery stock and pricing, log care events
- Catalog/UI: Plant nursery inventory and saved plant collection rendered as A2UI cards and care schedule tables
- Image gen: Plant growth visualizations, room styling with plants, and care/diagnosis illustrations
- Sandbox: Custom watering schedule calculations and fertilizer dilution ratios

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for watering and fertilizer calculations, Cloud Trace
